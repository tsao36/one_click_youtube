📘 How to get Google Cloud OAuth Credentials for your channel (Step-by-step Guide)
  1. Go to Google Cloud Console and create or select a project.
  2. Navigate to APIs & Services > Library, search for YouTube Data API v3, and click Enable.
  3. Go to APIs & Services > OAuth consent screen:
    • Select External and fill in App Name (e.g. AI Video Creator) and your email.
    • In the Test users section, add your YouTube Google Account email.
  4. Go to APIs & Services > Credentials:
    • Click Create Credentials > OAuth client ID.
    • Under Application type, select Desktop app.
    • Click Create, then click Download JSON.
  5. Click the 📁 Upload client_secrets.json button above and select your downloaded file.
  6. Click Sign In & Connect YouTube Channel to authenticate your channel!
